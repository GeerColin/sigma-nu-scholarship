# Analytics

Chair/Admin analytics cover chapter GPA trend, member trend, course trend, weekly on-time/late/missing rates, study-hour assignment and completion, alerts, and filters by semester/week/status. Members see only their own trends and never rankings or chapter averages.

Charts use accessible summaries and do not rely on color alone. Aggregate queries are chapter-scoped and server-side. Historical values use stored snapshots, not current mutable course or policy configuration. Small cohorts avoid unnecessary identity exposure in exports or chart labels.
